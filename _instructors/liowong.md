Lio Wong is a Stanford Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence Postdoctoral Fellow working with the Cognitive Tools Lab and completed their PhD in Brain and Cognitive Sciences in July 2024, advised by Josh Tenenbaum and Jacob Andreas. Lio's research asks  how people understand and learn from language, combining theory-driven cognitive experiments with formal computational tools, including structured probabilistic models of cognition, program synthesis, and machine learning approaches.
