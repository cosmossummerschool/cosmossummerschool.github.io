Dr. Alexandra Witt is a post-doctoral researcher at the RIKEN’s Center for Brain Science, in the Computational Group Dynamics (COGNAC) Collaboration Unit. She is interested in the individual processing of social information. Combining behavioural experiments, computational modelling, and cognitive neuroscience, she investigates the mechanisms underlying social learning and collective decision-making, with a focus on when social interaction improves or impairs collective performance.
